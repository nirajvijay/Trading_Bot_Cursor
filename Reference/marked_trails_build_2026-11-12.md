# ARB marked-stock trails: build and parity report (2026-11-12)

Branch `feat/arb-marked-trails` (from `origin/production` d629c50). Code only. Not deployed, no server or settings change.

## What is built
- **One switch:** `ArbSettings.marked_trails_enabled`, default **off**. It is plumbed through `arb_session_config`, the engine CLI (`--arb-marked-trails`), the launcher, the desk API and `execution_settings.json` (key `arb_marked_trails_enabled`; missing means off).
- **Drop flag:** `marked_trails_drop_below_actual` (`--arb-marked-trails-drop-below-actual`) drops HINDUNILVR and HINDZINC, the two stocks flagged `below_actual` in the table.
- **Rules table:** `config/marked_trails.json`, generated by `scripts/build_marked_trails.py` from `plan_bt.py`.
  - 53 stocks: 49 buy rules and 6 sell rules.
  - Levels are `levels()` over the 3-year cache (2023-11-09..2026-11-09), rounded to 2 dp. LTM (1.17/1.25) and DMART (1.17/1.30) are fixed.
  - Modes: `level_floor` for LODHA and DMART buys, `level_cap` (0.5%) for the LTM sell.
  - Every symbol is in the Nifty 100 universe; a test checks this.
- **`engine_marked_trails.py`** holds the pure functions: the table loader, `check_entry` (rules 1 and 2), the trail fold (rule 3, every mode, decimal maths, tick-rounded away from the market), `resolve_trail_stop` and `walk_trail` (the backtest walk).

### Engine (`engine_core`)
- **ORB and GUC entry gate.** It runs after the window and stop checks and before the budget.
  - Unmarked stock: `arb_marked_unmarked_stock`. The ORB slot stays empty, because the detector already fixed the top 3 and the engine never pulls in a replacement.
  - Entry beyond the level: `arb_marked_outside_level`.
  - No 09:15 bar: `arb_marked_open_unknown`. The engine never guesses an open.
  - Table unreadable: `arb_marked_table_unavailable`, and every ORB/GUC entry is skipped.
  - The funnel is not filtered. It is off live.
- **Plan stamped at entry.** Level, open and rule go into `extra.arb.marked_trail`, so a restart or a table edit cannot change an open trade's rule.
- **Trail after the touch.**
  - It is folded from completed 1-minute bars after the entry minute, the same reader and cache pattern as the lock rule.
  - The first write after the touch **replaces** the stop. That can loosen it below the lock stop, or even below the initial stop. After that it only tightens.
  - Writes go through `move_stop`, so the 20-modification cancel-and-replace applies. The pending marker covers unclear responses.
  - A target at or through the market is **sold at market** through `flatten` (reason `marked_trail_through_market`), because Kite would refuse that stop.
- **Halts.** Nothing changed here: `_stop_movable` and the sleeve halt flatten trailed trades like any other. An ORB halt flattens ORB only (PR #67); a trailed GUC trade keeps trailing. A test covers this.
- **Desk.** `arb_snapshot().marked_trails` shows enabled, table date, stock count, error and how many trades are trailing. Reason labels were added in `format.ts`.

### Replay
`arb_replay.replay_day(..., marked_book=)`, with the flag on, applies the live rules to ORB:
- unmarked and out-of-level trades are skipped before the budget;
- the trail switches only on a bar that completed before the normal exit;
- stops are tick-rounded and the square-off is at the 14:50 open;
- the day controls run on the trailed paths, and a halt flattens trailed trades.

With the flag off the replay is unchanged. The golden tests pass.

## Points for NJ
1. **More open risk than budgeted.**
   - After the touch, a wide trail can sit below the entry and below the initial stop. Examples: TRENT 4%, BPCL 2.5%, IOC 1.9%, IRFC 1.7%.
   - That is how the research did it. `position_live_risk` then rises, which shrinks room for new ORB trades.
2. **Trail through the market.** A stop that would already be through the market is sold at market. The research fills these at the next bar's open.
3. **GUC is not in `arb_replay`.** Its live-faithful number is the overlay with live conventions. GUC has no halt in the research table.
